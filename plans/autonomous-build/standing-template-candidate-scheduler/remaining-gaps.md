# Remaining gaps

- No fresh exact LIVE campaign authorization, one-time window,
  execution-scoped grant, lease or harmless live session/content input exists.
- ORCA executable 1.4.229 is available locally; real final-submit activation remains
  uncertified.
- Independent real publication discovery, permalink/post-ID matching and a
  canonical VERIFIED/PUBLISHED receipt remain unproven.
- Template derivation is deterministic and approved-template bounded; it does
  not provide production model generation, adaptive variants or content-fatigue
  control.
- Malformed templates are skipped per candidate. A future policy decision may
  choose to quarantine an entire authorization when any sibling template is
  malformed; current per-candidate fail-closed behavior is covered and does not
  broaden authority.
- Standing delegation still depends on an exact campaign-level authorization;
  the scheduler never invents or broadens authority.
