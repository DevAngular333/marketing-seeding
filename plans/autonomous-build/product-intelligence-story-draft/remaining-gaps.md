# Remaining gaps

- No production model/provider or autonomous CTA/positioning strategy is
  connected to this derivation.
- Story derivation is not yet wired into the standing queue; a future seam must
  define when a story is eligible for candidate/template scheduling.
- Product intelligence still depends on bounded local repository inspection and
  caller-supplied claims where heuristics cannot safely derive a field.
- Live LinkedIn publication, independent publication discovery and metrics
  remain blocked by the existing authorization/session/ORCA lane.
