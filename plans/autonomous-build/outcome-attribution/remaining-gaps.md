# Remaining gaps

- No live metric, interaction, reply or conversion evidence exists in the
  canonical runtime.
- Attribution is advisory and does not yet drive strategy rerouting or a
  next-best-action authority decision.
- Account/session selection, failover and multi-account attribution remain
  unimplemented.
- Campaign-level conversions without an explicit `postId` remain unassigned.
- Real LinkedIn publication and ORCA final-submit certification remain blocked
  by the existing external authorization/session blocker.
