# Remaining Gaps

- Product Factory still does not run arbitrary product builds, renderers, package managers or scripts; a real sandbox is required before expanding that boundary.
- Artifact promotion is operator-triggered and does not itself select an artifact, attach it to a draft, authorize publication, or prove external publication.
- No live LinkedIn publication/verification proof exists. Fresh exact campaign/account/profile/target/content authority and a usable ORCA session are still absent.
- Upstream `SYSTEM_AGENTIC_AI` handoff remains DRY_RUN-only.
- Account selection, login/session recovery, multi-account failover, audience discovery and outcome-driven strategy remain partial or unimplemented.
