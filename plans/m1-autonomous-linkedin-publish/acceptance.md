# Milestone 1 acceptance

## Goal

Prove one fresh humanless LinkedIn execution from an authorized job through JEV/ORCA final submit, semantic activation, platform acceptance, independent publication discovery and canonical `PUBLISHED`/`CONFIRMED` state.

## Acceptance criteria

- A1: Fresh execution, active exact campaign authorization, one-time window, live grant and unique lease bind the same job/account/profile/target/content/visibility.
- A2: Durable submit intent is persisted before exactly one autonomous final submit action.
- A3: Transport, semantic activation, platform acceptance and publication verification remain separate evidence layers.
- A4: Independent verifier proves post ID/permalink, author, normalized content and publication time window before `VERIFIED`/`PUBLISHED`.
- A5: Historical UNKNOWN executions remain unchanged and no uncertain submission is redispatched.
- A6: Full existing Node suite passes with zero failures and process exits normally.

## Constraints

- C1: Do not add a dashboard approval step or require operator final click.
- C2: Do not publish through another platform, retry historical executions, or manually edit canonical state.
- C3: Do not print credentials, cookies, tokens or session secrets.
