# Next roadmap (maximum five phases)

This is a recommendation only. No phase is implemented by this audit.

## NEXT 1 — Controlled live LinkedIn proof and transport certification

**Goal:** One fresh harmless authorized LinkedIn execution completes autonomous submit, semantic activation, acceptance, independent discovery and normalized `VERIFIED` receipt.

**Why now:** It is the only blocker to proving the current execution vertical and determines whether ORCA can be used for irreversible action.

**Dependency:** Fresh exact campaign authorization, account/profile, content, target, policy, one-time window, live grant, readiness and an exclusive live lane.

**Acceptance:** One new execution ID; one durable intent; one final click; positive activation; acceptance; permalink/post ID; author/content/time match; canonical `PUBLISHED`/`CONFIRMED`; no duplicate; historical UNKNOWN unchanged.

**Risk:** Platform UI/session ambiguity. Fail closed and quarantine after intent.

## NEXT 2 — Product handoff, intelligence and artifact lineage

**Goal:** System-Agentic input becomes a versioned domain handoff, then repository inspection produces evidence-grounded product answers and persistent factory artifacts.

**Why now:** Distribution decisions are unsafe without a trustworthy product definition and evidence lineage.

**Dependency:** Frozen core boundary, current local handoff/intelligence/factory seams.

**Acceptance:** Product/repo input yields machine-readable WHAT/PROBLEM/BETTER/AUDIENCE, claim graph, unsupported claims, reproducible artifact run, hashes and dashboard projection.

**Risk:** Overclaiming or executing unsafe repository commands. Keep allowlists, path containment and redaction.

## NEXT 3 — Authorized discovery and strategy

**Goal:** Continuous observation becomes normalized opportunities with audience/channel/community eligibility and explainable next-best-action decisions.

**Why now:** The current discovery module is useful only as a caller-supplied foundation.

**Dependency:** Product intelligence, policy registry, account/platform policy and durable scheduler.

**Acceptance:** Observe → normalize → deduplicate → classify → score → policy-check → choose action; no duplicate processing; every decision has evidence and a reason; no action creates authority by itself.

**Risk:** Platform access limits, spam risk and stale community rules. Preserve blocked/unknown states.

## NEXT 4 — Production content, account runtime, metrics and learning

**Goal:** Generate evidence-grounded channel/context variants, select an eligible authorized account/session, collect outcomes and adapt strategy.

**Why now:** Current drafts, single-account reuse and metrics collectors do not close the autonomous loop.

**Dependency:** NEXT 1–3, secure credential references, provider policy and verified outcome data.

**Acceptance:** No per-post approval during active bounded authority; wrong-account and duplicate protections; metrics provenance; strategy changes linked to observed outcomes; unknown data never becomes a positive signal.

**Risk:** Credential/session isolation, provider quotas and model hallucination. Require bounded authority and claim grounding.

## NEXT 5 — Incremental platform certification and 24/7 hardening

**Goal:** Add one platform at a time and prove restart, browser recovery, network/rate-limit handling, durable resume, no duplicate side effects and dashboard Mission Control.

**Why now:** Multi-platform breadth should follow one proven vertical rather than precede it.

**Dependency:** A verified LinkedIn vertical, account runtime, metrics model and platform-specific adapter contracts.

**Acceptance:** Each certified platform has readiness, exact execution, acceptance, independent verification, idempotency, recovery and observability evidence; long-running soak has no state corruption or duplicate external actions.

**Risk:** Platform policy changes and live account restrictions. Isolate unhealthy accounts and never bypass challenges.

## Shortest path to the bounded North Star

```text
fresh standing authority
  -> one real LinkedIn publish/verify proof
  -> freeze execution contract
  -> upstream product handoff
  -> automated product intelligence + artifact lineage
  -> authorized discovery + strategy
  -> grounded content + account/session runtime
  -> metrics + learning
  -> one additional platform at a time
```
